THE ENEMIES:
(the images will be added soon)


Space Invaders - Level 1,2 (maybe 3 and 4 too):
These little guys are the first enemies you will encounter. They are really weak, and are also basic. They act just like they did in the arcade game, from moving step by step to changing colors when they get closer!


(W.I.P.) Child Soldiers - Level 2:
A little bit trickier than the previous enemies, these guys throw a Lolly (slang for lollipop) that deals moderate damage, then quickly go out of bounds! They will reappear with fresh ammo though.


Aeolosaurus - Level 1,2,3,4:
These gliding tanks shoot nothing, but they are still dangerous! Because of their immense weight, they will deal heavy damage if they touch your platform.


Sewer Gators - Level 3,4:
These dangerous looking reptiles will eat other enemies, but they aren't helpful. Because each time they eat something, they drop a tooth, which will fall and stab your platform! Try to avoid those teeth at all costs, because they deal a ton of damage.


Mecha Gators - Level 3,4:
These battered flying robots will drop a barrage of gears, bolts and other mechanical parts as they sweep across the screen. While they don't deal much damage, they might still overwhelm you.


(W.I.P.) Majesters - Level 4:
As in Mage + Jester, these weird guys will ready a grim spell when they appear. If you don't kill them quickly, they will rapidly shoot fireballs, which will form a wall.


Each enemy also has a special variant, like an UFO, or an Ankylosaurus, and much more! (much as in three)
